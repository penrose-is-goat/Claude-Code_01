FOMC decision dates — verify against federalreserve.gov; these drive the month-split math
Methodology (same as CME FedWatch / Investing.com Fed Rate Monitor): each futures price implies the average fed funds rate for its month (100 − price). Meeting months are split at the decision date and solved for the post-meeting rate. Outcome probabilities chain meeting-to-meeting through a binomial tree on the 25bp target-range grid, so distributions widen for later meetings exactly as on CME's tool. Tentative dates are marked with an asterisk.
